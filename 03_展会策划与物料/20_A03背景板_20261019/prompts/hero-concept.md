Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: abstract hero artwork for a premium Chinese industrial-software exhibition backwall, not the full poster.
Create an exceptionally refined, minimal, wide horizontal DATA RIBBON visual. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no QR code.
Canvas 2400 x 900 pixels, broad 8:3 horizontal composition. Absolutely uniform solid deep navy background #0F172A (no vignette).
Subject: on the left, 10–14 intentionally composed slender slate-blue process-data filaments, gently irregular and separated, flowing left-to-right. These align and merge into one beautifully disciplined bundled ribbon, with a translucent cyan-blue envelope around the tightly grouped central curves. The right half rises in a single graceful broad crest, then settles into a precise horizontal continuation, conveying raw industrial process data becoming meaningful production knowledge. A memorable sculptural curve, not a chart, not a random waveform. Smooth continuous contour lines, subtly varying weights, flat vector-like linework and translucent overlapping bands. Make the visual beautiful at a distance. Maximum width occupies 90%, height 65%, ample breathing room.
Palette: #0F172A navy, #1E3A5F desaturated blue, #94A3B8 silver-slate strokes, restrained #38BDF8 cyan edges. Cyan should not fill the canvas. Main visual sits center vertically, anchored right. Small, restrained edge luminosity only, no neon halo.
Style: Swiss corporate editorial art direction, precision industrial design, elegant mathematical calligraphy, ultra-clean premium vector aesthetic, matte finish. No grids, axes, dots, particles, stars, circuit boards, devices, factory photos, stock icons, 3D objects, gradients across the background, lens flares, glowing brains, stairs, arrows or UI panels. The artwork must read as one purposeful graphic gesture.
